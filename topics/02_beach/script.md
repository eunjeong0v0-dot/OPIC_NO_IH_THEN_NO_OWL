# 해변 가기 (Beach)

## 정보 (오순 님 실제 경험)
- 좋아하는 해변: 경포대
- 가는 방법: KTX 타고 감
- 언제: 이번 여름
- 좋아하는 이유(메인포인트): 경치가 너무 예쁨
  - 바다 색깔이 초록색/파랑색으로 계속 달라 보이는 게 매력적
  - 모래가 빵처럼 갈색빛을 띠는 게 마음에 듦
  - 보고 있으면 평화로워짐
- 가는 빈도: 1년에 1번 정도
- 함께 가는 사람: 남자친구
- 이동 수단: KTX 또는 차
- 가서 하는 일: 바다 보이는 예쁜 카페에서 구경하기

---

## Q1. "Tell me about your favorite beach. What does it look like, and why do you like it?" [Description]

Um, my favorite beach is Gyeongpodae. [a: Intro]

I mean, honestly, the main reason I love it so much is because, you know, the scenery is just so pretty. [a: Main Point]

Like, the color of the sea keeps changing — sometimes it looks green, and sometimes it looks blue. I don't know, but I just find that so attractive. [c: AL표현 — 색깔 변화 디테일]

And, um, the sand has this brownish color too, kind of like fresh bread. It's such a small detail, but I really like that about it. [c: AL표현 — Simile "like fresh bread"]

So, whenever I just sit there and look at the sea, I feel so peaceful. [b: filler "so", detail]

That's why Gyeongpodae is my favorite beach. That's about it. [a: Conclusion]

---

## Q2. "When was the last time you went to the beach? Tell me about that experience." [Past Experience]

Um, the most recent time I went to the beach was, uh, this summer. [a: Intro]

I went to Gyeongpodae, and, you know, I took the KTX to get there. [d: Past Experience — 이동수단 디테일]

When I got there, I just sat and looked at the sea for a while, and, um, it was exactly how I remembered it — the sea was, like, switching between green and blue, and the sand was this brownish color, kind of like bread. [c: AL표현 — 반복으로 일관성 강조]

And, I mean, just watching it made me feel so relaxed and peaceful. [b: filler "I mean", "so"]

So yeah, that's the last time I went to the beach, and it was such a nice trip. [a: Conclusion]

---

## Q3. "How often do you go to the beach? Who do you usually go with, and what do you do there?" [Habit]

Um, I'd say I go to the beach, like, once a year, maybe. [a: Intro / b: filler "like"]

I usually go with my boyfriend, and, uh, we either take the KTX or just drive there, depending on the weather. [d: Habit — 함께 가는 사람 + 이동수단]

And, you know, once we get there, we don't really swim or anything. [b: filler "you know"]

Instead, we usually just find this, um, pretty cafe near the beach, and we sit there and look at the view. [c: AL표현 — "instead"로 전환]

I mean, we just order some coffee and, like, spend hours just staring at the sea. [b: filler "I mean", "like" + detail word "just"]

It's such a simple thing, but, um, it's honestly one of my favorite ways to spend time with him. [b: filler "um"]

So yeah, that's pretty much how we spend our time whenever we go to the beach. [a: Conclusion]

---

## 포인트
- Main point("경치가 예쁘다")를 초반에 바로 던지고, 색깔 변화/모래색을 디테일로 전개
- "like fresh bread" simile로 감각적인 묘사 추가 (AL 표현)
- Q1(Description)과 Q2(Past Experience)에서 같은 디테일(바다색/모래색)을 재활용해서 암기 부담 최소화
- Q3(Habit)은 main point 없이 "빈도 → 함께가는 사람 → 하는 일(카페에서 구경)" 순서로 디테일만 이어감
- 필러(um, you know, I mean, so)를 문장 중간중간 배치
