# Recycling — 표현 & 4유형 문장

출처: 오픽노잼 유튜브 "100문장으로 끝내는 오픽 문형" 영상 스크립트

## meticulous
- Description: People in my country are meticulous about recycling.
- Habit: I try to be meticulous when I recycle at home.
- Past Experience: I remember once I was meticulous about recycling, but the truck didn't come that day.
- Comparison: These days you need to be meticulous when it comes to recycling.

## down to the last detail
- Description: Rules are explained down to the last detail.
- Habit: At home, I follow the guide down to the last detail.
- Past Experience: My teacher explained recycling down to the last detail.
- Comparison: These days, recycling is regulated down to the last detail.

## compost
- Description: Food waste is used as compost in my country.
- Habit: I separate food waste to make compost.
- Past Experience: At school, I once made compost from food waste.
- Comparison: Before, people didn't make compost at home.

## go the extra mile
- Description: In my country, some people go the extra mile by washing all the bottles before recycling.
- Habit: I always go the extra mile by checking the labels before throwing things away.
- Past Experience: One time I went the extra mile by separating everything, but the truck didn't come.
- Comparison: In the past, people didn't go the extra mile with recycling.

## vinyl
- Description: In my country, people separate vinyl bags from other kinds of trash.
- Habit: At home, I always recycle vinyl by putting it in a clear bag.
- Past Experience: When I was young, I didn't know vinyl had to be recycled separately.
- Comparison: In the past, people often threw vinyl away instead of recycling it.
