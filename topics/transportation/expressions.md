# Transportation — 표현 & 4유형 문장

출처: 오픽노잼 유튜브 "100문장으로 끝내는 오픽 문형" 영상 스크립트

## introverted / extroverted
- Description: I'm introverted, so I like quiet bus rides.
- Habit: I'm introverted, so I don't usually talk on the bus.
- Past Experience: One time I sat next to an extroverted person on the bus.
- Comparison: But these days I'm quite introverted.

## when it comes to something
- Description: Safety is important when it comes to transportation.
- Habit: When it comes to commuting, I take the subway every day.
- Past Experience: When it came to commuting, I walked to school.
- Comparison: But these days, when it comes to commuting, I choose the subway.

## public transportation
- Description: Public transportation is very good in my city.
- Habit: I use public transportation every day to go to work.
- Past Experience: I once took public transportation and it was crowded.
- Comparison: But these days I use public transportation all the time.

## fare
- Description: The bus fare in my city is quite cheap.
- Habit: I paid $2 for the bus fare every day.
- Past Experience: I once was annoyed because I didn't have enough for the bus fare.
- Comparison: But these days the subway fare is too expensive.

## subway line
- Description: My city has numerous subway lines.
- Habit: I usually check the subway lines on my phone before I go to work.
- Past Experience: I once got lost because I took the wrong subway line.
- Comparison: But these days, so many new subway lines have been built.
