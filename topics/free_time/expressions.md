# Free Time — 표현 & 4유형 문장

출처: 오픽노잼 유튜브 "100문장으로 끝내는 오픽 문형" 영상 스크립트

## look forward to
- Description: I look forward to hanging out with my friends during my free time.
- Habit: I tend to look forward to the weekend when I'm at work.
- Past Experience: I was once so sick and looked forward to a speedy recovery.
- Comparison: But these days I tend to look forward to major holidays like Christmas.

## stay in
- Description: I truly believe staying in is the best way to spend your free time.
- Habit: Staying in and doing nothing is how I usually spend my free time.
- Past Experience: I once had some free time and decided to just stay in.
- Comparison: In the past, I preferred staying in to hanging out.

## hang out
- Description: I love hanging out with my friends during my free time.
- Habit: These days I don't have enough free time to hang out with my friends.
- Past Experience: I have this vivid memory of hanging out with my friends during my free time.
- Comparison: But now I prefer hanging out with my family when I have free time.

## recharge
- Description: Free time is the only time that I can fully recharge.
- Habit: I like to recharge at home whenever I have free time.
- Past Experience: Last Saturday I decided to recharge at a cafe.
- Comparison: Back when I was younger I would read books to recharge during my free time.

## go for a walk
- Description: Going for a walk is one of my favorite ways to spend free time.
- Habit: I always go for a walk whenever I have free time.
- Past Experience: I went for a walk last night with my wife and it was a great experience.
- Comparison: But now I've realized that going for a walk is a nice way to spend free time.
