# Technology — 표현 & 4유형 문장

출처: 오픽노잼 유튜브 "100문장으로 끝내는 오픽 문형" 영상 스크립트

## invent
- Description: Whoever invented AirPods, I really appreciate them.
- Habit: I usually enjoy looking up videos about great inventions on YouTube.
- Past Experience: Engineers invented a lot of new machines to make life easier.
- Comparison: But these days we're surrounded by so many amazing inventions.

## rehearse
- Description: I've realized that doing a camera rehearsal is really important.
- Habit: As a YouTuber, I always rehearse before shooting.
- Past Experience: I once skipped a dress rehearsal before a live company event and I totally regretted it.
- Comparison: But now I try to rehearse every single time before an important event.

## impressive
- Description: I think electronics these days are pretty impressive.
- Habit: I often buy the newest tech products that really impress me.
- Past Experience: When I was in middle school, I thought MP3 players were extremely impressive.
- Comparison: But now some gadgets like MP3 players aren't impressive anymore.

## usable
- Description: My laptop is pretty cheap, but it's still very usable.
- Habit: I check my headphones almost every morning to make sure they're usable throughout the day.
- Past Experience: I thought it would be usable, but it turned out to be completely broken.
- Comparison: So now I always charge my wireless mouse to make sure it's usable when I need it.

## value
- Description: I value my iPad more than any other gadget I own.
- Habit: I always bring my MacBook with me because I really value it.
- Past Experience: My MP3 player was the most valuable thing I had when I was younger.
- Comparison: These days I really value how quickly technology is developing.
