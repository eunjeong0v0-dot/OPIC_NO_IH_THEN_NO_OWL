## on a daily basis
- Description: On a daily basis, the cafe near my home is full of people in the morning.
- Habit: I go to a cafe on a daily basis to get a latte before work.
- Past Experience: In the past, I studied at a cafe on a daily basis.
- Comparison: But these days, I drink coffee on a daily basis.

## fanatic
- Description: I'm a coffee fanatic.
- Habit: I'm not a fanatic, but I drink coffee every day.

- Comparison: But nowadays I'm a coffee fanatic.

## sleek
- Description: The cafe is sleek with modern lights.

## ritual
- Description: For many people, coffee is a morning ritual.
- Habit: My ritual is to drink an Americano before work.

## neighborhood
